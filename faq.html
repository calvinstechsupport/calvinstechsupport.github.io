Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about pricing, repairs, safety and how to get started.

Pricing and process

Do you charge for diagnosis?

Basic diagnostics start from R80. The price is confirmed before any deeper repair work begins.

What does “starting from” mean?

It is the lowest price for that kind of job, not a final quote. The final amount depends on the actual fault, the time it takes, the parts needed and how complex it is. I confirm it with you before paid work starts. See the prices page.

Do I need to buy the parts myself?

Sometimes, depending on availability. Parts are separate from labour either way. I will advise which part is needed before you buy anything.

How long does a repair take?

Simple jobs can be quick. Parts, deeper diagnostics or complex faults take longer. I can give you a realistic idea once I know the fault.

Do you offer student prices?

The service is student-run and I keep prices as affordable as I can. The starting prices are on the prices page.

Devices and repairs

Do you repair phones and laptops?

I help with phone, laptop and desktop troubleshooting, basic repairs, software problems and upgrades. See what I can help with.

Do you install Windows?

Yes. I can help with Windows installation, drivers, updates and basic software setup. I will talk you through backups first, because reinstalling can erase files.

Can you remove viruses?

Yes. I can check for malware, remove suspicious software and unwanted browser add-ons, and advise how to avoid it happening again.

Do you help with WiFi?

Yes. Router setup, basic speed checks and connection troubleshooting. If the fault is on your internet provider’s side, I can point you in the right direction but I can’t fix their network.

Can you recover my files?

It depends on the condition of the storage device, and I can’t promise it. Send the details first so I can advise honestly. If the files matter, stop using the device until we have talked.

Can you repair components on a board?

I inspect boards and small components and give advice before anything is replaced. Whether a board-level repair is possible depends on the fault, and I will say plainly what is realistic before any work.

Before you message

What should I send you?

The device make and model, what happened before the problem started, clear photos or a short video, and your area in Cape Town. The contact page has a request builder that puts this into a WhatsApp message for you.

Do I need to bring my charger?

Yes, if possible. A charger helps confirm whether the problem is the device, the battery, the port or the power adapter.

What should I never send?

Passwords, PINs, one-time codes, recovery codes or banking details. I will never ask for them in a message.

Areas and booking

Which areas do you cover?

For now: Cape Town CBD, Observatory, Parow, Bellville and Belhar. Message first so we can confirm the arrangement and timing.

How do I book?

WhatsApp 068 394 7321 with your device model, the problem, and photos if possible. Nothing is booked until we have confirmed the details together.

Still have a question?

Ask on WhatsApp and I will answer honestly, including when the answer is that a repair isn’t worth it.
