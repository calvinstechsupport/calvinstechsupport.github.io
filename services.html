What I won’t promise

Honest limits are part of the service. If something isn’t worth repairing, I will say so before any paid work.

  • Data recovery. It depends on the condition of the storage device. I advise honestly and don’t promise results.
  • Guaranteed board-level repairs. Component-level work depends on the fault, and I will tell you what is realistic.
  • Fixing your internet provider’s network. I can point you in the right direction.
  • A final price before I know the fault. Starting prices are a guide. Your price is confirmed before paid work begins.
