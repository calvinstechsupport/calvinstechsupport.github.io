Privacy

Privacy note.

A plain-language explanation of what happens when you use this static website.

Support request form

The form on this website runs in your browser and prepares a WhatsApp message. It does not submit the form fields to a website database or store them on this website. Nothing is sent to WhatsApp until you choose to continue in WhatsApp.

Appearance preferences

The website uses your browser's local storage to remember display choices such as light/dark mode and accent colour. These settings are not account credentials and are not sent to the site owner.

What not to send

Do not include passwords, one-time PINs, banking information, recovery codes or other secrets in a support request. If a device needs attention, describe the symptoms instead.

Third-party services

When you open WhatsApp, its own service and privacy terms apply to information you choose to send there. This static site does not control WhatsApp's data handling.

This is a practical website notice, not legal advice. It should be reviewed if analytics, advertising, payments, bookings or any backend/data collection are added later.
