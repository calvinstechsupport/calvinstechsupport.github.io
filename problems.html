Common problems, in plain language

Tell me the problem, not just the device. Find yours below to see what usually causes it, what I check first and what to send. This is guidance, not a diagnosis; a proper check confirms the fault.

  • Slow laptop or PC

    What it is often caused by

    • Too many programs starting with Windows
    • A full or ageing drive (an SSD upgrade often helps)
    • Not enough RAM for how you use it
    • Unwanted software or browser add-ons

    What I check first

    What starts with Windows, how full and how old the drive is, and how much memory there is.

    What to send me

    The make and model, and whether it was always slow or got slower recently.

    Starting point

    Diagnostics from R80. Cleanup or upgrade is quoted after the check. How pricing works

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Windows won’t start

    What it is often caused by

    • A failed update or damaged system files
    • A failing drive
    • Loose or faulty memory or cables
    • A power or charger problem

    What I check first

    Whether it powers on, shows an error or beeps, plus the health of the drive.

    What to send me

    A photo of the error on screen and what happened just before it stopped. If you have files you can’t lose, say so before anything is reinstalled.

    Starting point

    Diagnostics from R80. Any repair is quoted after the check. How pricing works

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Virus or pop-ups in the browser

    What it is often caused by

    • An unwanted browser extension
    • A website that was given permission to send notifications
    • Software bundled with a free download
    • Actual malware

    What I check first

    Installed programs, browser extensions, start-up items and a malware scan.

    What to send me

    A photo of the pop-up, which browser it is, and roughly when it began. Don’t enter passwords on pages that appeared unexpectedly.

    Starting point

    Virus removal from R150. How pricing works

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Phone not charging

    What it is often caused by

    • A worn cable or charger
    • Lint or dirt in the charging port
    • A worn battery
    • A damaged port or charging circuit

    What I check first

    A different cable and charger first, then the port and the battery.

    What to send me

    The phone model, whether it charges at an angle, slowly or not at all, and a photo of the port.

    Starting point

    Phone check-up from R80. Any part is quoted separately. How pricing works

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Battery draining fast

    What it is often caused by

    • An ageing battery
    • Apps running in the background
    • Weak signal or screen settings
    • A software fault

    What I check first

    Battery health, which apps use the most power, and settings.

    What to send me

    The phone or laptop model, how old it is, and how long the battery lasts now compared with before.

    Starting point

    Phone check-up from R80. How pricing works

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • White, black or flickering screen

    What it is often caused by

    • A loose or damaged display cable
    • A cracked or failed screen
    • A graphics or motherboard fault
    • A driver or software problem

    What I check first

    Whether it powers on, an external monitor test where possible, and the connections.

    What to send me

    A photo or short video of the screen, whether lights or fans come on, and whether it was dropped or got wet. Some faults are not worth repairing and I will say so before any work.

    Starting point

    Diagnostics from R80. A repair is quoted once the fault is known. How pricing works

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • WiFi connected but no internet

    What it is often caused by

    • A router that needs a restart or a settings fix
    • DNS or address settings
    • Weak signal
    • A fault on the internet provider’s side

    What I check first

    Whether other devices connect, the router settings and the signal.

    What to send me

    The router make, whether other devices work, and when it started. If the fault is the provider’s, I can point you in the right direction but cannot repair their network.

    Starting point

    WiFi and router setup from R150. How pricing works

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Drivers or software errors

    What it is often caused by

    • Missing or outdated drivers
    • Software that doesn’t suit your version of Windows
    • A problem after a Windows update

    What I check first

    Device Manager, update history and which program is failing.

    What to send me

    The exact error message (a photo is fine) and what you were doing when it appeared.

    Starting point

    Software setup from R150. How pricing works

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Need a RAM or SSD upgrade

    What it is often caused by

    • Not every laptop can take more memory or a bigger drive
    • The right part depends on the exact model
    • Parts are bought separately

    What I check first

    What your model supports and whether the upgrade will actually fix the slowness.

    What to send me

    The exact model (it is on the sticker or in Windows settings) and what you want to be faster.

    Starting point

    RAM or SSD upgrade help from R200, parts not included. How pricing works

    Ask about this on WhatsApp

  • Setup help for a new device or app

    What it is often caused by

    • A new phone, laptop or router to set up
    • Apps or accounts you are unsure how to configure

    What I check first

    What you need set up and how you use the device.

    What to send me

    The device model and what you want it to do. Never send passwords, PINs or one-time codes in a message.

    Starting point

    Software setup from R150, or a custom quote. How pricing works

Before you send your device

  • Back up anything you can’t lose. If it won’t start, tell me before anything is reinstalled.
  • Take a photo of any error message or pop-up.
  • Note what happened just before the problem began (a drop, spilled liquid, an update, a power cut).
  • Bring the charger if you can.
  • Never send passwords, PINs, one-time codes or banking details in a message.
